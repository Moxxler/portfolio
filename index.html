RENEWABLE ENERGY & POWER GRID SIMULATIONS

Solarpark Simulation

Simulating east-west single axis solar trackers by calculating their yearly yield accurate to the minute through the use of vectors, rotation matrices, and Test Reference Year data measured in Hamburg, Germany. Programmed in C.


PandaPower Grid Injection

Simulating the injection of the simulated solarpark into the pre-assembled german MV-Oberrhein grid using PandaPower to inspect line loading and bus voltages on peak generation days. Programmed in Python.
